# Training provenance versus evaluation reproduction

This audit concerns the scope of later causal and generalization claims. It does not change the running evaluation, launch training, or assert benchmark contamination.

The saved SSD paper v2 (research-state/X02-visuals/SSD.txt, Appendix C.1) describes roughly 10,168 whitespace-normalized, exactly deduplicated rSTARcoder problem statements, one self-generated response per prompt, and only minimal degeneracy filtering. The released Qwen-based checkpoint card identifies training temperature 1.6 and evaluation temperature 1.1. Those statements are the recipe evidence for the released model; we have not obtained its exact training-example manifest. [SSD paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01193v2), [checkpoint card](https://huggingface.co/apple/SimpleSD-4B-instruct).

The pinned author source at 2637d2021f1bc523385b48a1f88ea9aa4812b0a9 loads every row returned by load_dataset, optionally takes a leading limit, formats each row and generates responses. There is no prompt deduplication in that path. The shipped YAML uses temperature 1.5 and a bottom-10-percent response-length filter. The code calculates this threshold from character counts, not model-token counts, and retains ties at the threshold. These settings are a runnable example, not sufficient evidence of the checkpoint's exact training procedure. Source bytes and hashes are preserved here; the parent directory's UPSTREAM-LICENSE applies. A search of all seven source/config/document files in the checkout found no deduplication references, but cannot rule out external preprocessing.

The [official dataset viewer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/microsoft/rStar-Coder/viewer/seed_sft) advertises approximately 592k seed_sft rows and exposes solution-bearing records, not an already established list of the paper's 10,168 unique training prompts. The [dataset documentation](https://huggingface.co/datasets/microsoft/rStar-Coder/blob/main/README.md) lists question, starter_code, response, code, verified and is_passed fields. In the captured SSD generation path, prompt construction reads question and starter_code, then uses newly generated output as the training target. The presence of reference fields in the source dataset is not evidence that SSD used those fields as targets or a correctness filter.

The [rSTARcoder paper v1, Section 3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21297v1#S3.SS3) reports 16-gram benchmark-overlap removal. Its stated evaluation uses LCB v5, August 2024–February 2025. This is a source-reported decontamination procedure, not our byte-level verification of the later SSD v6 subset or the exact training manifest. Neither absence nor presence of overlap with our 131 tasks has been established here.

Direct metadata requests from hn01 to the authorized HF mirror returned HTTP403 for dataset info and tree. The web-indexed official seed_sft listing shows roughly 12.1GB across 20 shards at revision 84f357c81c2fe6282150ea0e040db04e181ecfbe; we did not download those shards or substitute an unverified mirror dataset. The running model evaluation has no dependency on that retrieval. A later exact overlap audit should obtain the actual prompt manifest or read only question columns from a pinned source, normalize consistently and preserve exact versus approximate matching distinctions. Checking the entire seed pool would provide a candidate-overlap screen, not proof of membership in the released model's training set.

Practical consequence: finish evaluation of the released checkpoint with its documented decoder and appropriate controls. Do not call this a full training reproduction. If new training becomes scientifically justified, first bind prompt deduplication, filtering, weighting and the retained-example manifest; otherwise changes in those choices can masquerade as a mechanism effect. This provenance gap adds no experimental admission requirement and does not pause current work.
