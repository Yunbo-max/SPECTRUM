# Question-led experiment selection in B, C, D and E

Work Harness applies this guidance to scientific selection and phase review.
It complements phase-autonomy.md and overrides wording that treats existing
models, datasets, configurations or previous conclusions as mandatory choices.
Use the existing phase analysis and evidence records; no additional form, gate,
per-experiment approval or fixed model matrix is introduced.

## B memory is evidence to assess

Restore the user's actual research objective and constraints. Separate that scope
from inherited hypotheses, favored methods and experimental conveniences. B memory
retains sources, code, observations, uncertainty and exposure history. Neither
being remembered nor having passed an earlier review establishes applicability to
a new question. Check whether the earlier framing, interpretation and selected
population were wrong, incomplete or too narrow. Preserve valid knowledge while
refreshing relevant primary papers, official code and checked discussion leads.
Do not restart all literature work without a reason or treat cache availability,
completed setup or past expenditure as scientific support.

## C chooses the question and the informative setting

First identify the claim and rival explanations, including that the apparent
problem is absent or caused by the chosen model/task/protocol. Select the model
family/scale, dataset, task conditions, strong/simple comparator and evaluation
because their outcomes can distinguish those explanations. Explain consequential
selection and exclusion choices in the existing research analysis. Assess model
capability, task difficulty, floor/ceiling effects, population relevance, precision,
truncation, sampling, training objective, legal information and comparison budgets
when they can affect interpretation. Use actual hardware measurements for
feasibility; do not let the first runnable configuration define the research.

A small model can support a justified scoped claim. Low accuracy alone does not
establish a method defect. If capacity, family or dataset choice could explain a
finding, choose the comparison that resolves it, which may require another model
or stronger setting during C rather than deferring it automatically to E. No
universal minimum parameter count, seed count, dataset count or larger-is-better
rule applies. Positive and negative findings both inform selection; do not search
only for conditions where a preferred method wins or an existing method fails.

Decide scientific fit before engineering reuse. Reuse compatible official code,
environments, model/data caches and valid results after that decision. Acquire or
adapt different assets when needed. Model, task and setting choices remain
revisable within the authorized field and resources; changing them does not by
itself require a new agent or owner approval.

## D can revise C and abandon a favored mechanism

Treat C's supported problem as a scoped, revisable premise. Derive a mechanism,
map its mathematics to code and run meaningful experiments with strong/simple
alternatives. Select interventions from conditional predictions and falsifiers.
If the premise no longer fits or a simpler explanation suffices, return to the
relevant B/C work, revise the question or archive the mechanism. Do not require
a named project method, spectral idea or earlier proposal to succeed. Preserve
contradictory results and failed attempts. Reopening accepted scientific gates
uses the existing reviewed revisit route; ordinary unaccepted B/C/D reasoning
stays within the same sustained worker.

## E covers the surviving claim

Expand model scales/families, tasks, populations, controls, replications and cost
measurements to test the scope of the claim and its predicted boundaries. Breadth
is justified by what it can establish, not by filling a large Cartesian matrix.
Freeze confirmatory settings, retain fair information/tuning/compute budgets and
report practical effects with uncertainty. Keep C/D development exposure visible:
previously inspected test outcomes are not unseen independent confirmation merely
because the phase is now E. Reserve appropriate unexposed evaluation evidence.
E counterevidence can reopen C/D through the existing supported transition.

## Independent phase review

Review why the selected question and experimental objects are informative, not
just whether code ran or old assets were reused. Challenge inherited framing,
capacity/difficulty confounds, cherry-picked conditions, weak comparators and
unwarranted generalization. A cached model or a larger model is not automatically
adequate. Accept well-supported limited scope without imposing a model-count quota;
require the discriminating evidence actually needed for the claimed conclusion.
Keep scope changes inside the user's objective and authority. Preserve immutable
active protocols, job identity, original results and cumulative usage; apply new
selections to versioned successor work after reconciling existing jobs.
